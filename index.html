Good questions, straight answers

Frequently asked questions.

Do you help people who aren't American?

Yes. The website speaks especially to Americans planning a permanent move, but we welcome enquiries from other nationalities. Immigration rules and other legal requirements depend on individual nationality and circumstances; we do not determine eligibility or provide legal representation.

Can you get me a visa or AIMA appointment?

No. We offer general planning information and can help organize questions for qualified immigration professionals. We cannot guarantee visas, residence permits, appointment availability or decisions by Portuguese authorities. Always check current requirements with the relevant consulate and AIMA ↗.

Will you find, negotiate or sell me a home?

No. Our area and listing discussions are educational only. We don't broker real-estate transactions, introduce counterparties for a transaction as a mediation service, negotiate on your behalf, or replace a licensed property agent, surveyor or lawyer. Property mediation in Portugal requires appropriate licensing.

What happens after I request a service?

Once the business is fully launched, you'll receive written confirmation of scope, timing, price and payment instructions before any paid work begins. This first website version is a demo and does not submit enquiries or take payments yet.

Can you promise Portugal will be right for me?

No—and that’s the point. We'll talk openly about trade-offs and help you ask better questions. Final decisions remain yours, based on independent verification and specialist advice where appropriate.
