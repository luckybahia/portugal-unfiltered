The questions worth asking before you move.

A starting library of realistic considerations—not visa advice or a substitute for current official requirements.

⌂

Before you rent or buy

Photographs cannot tell you everything about everyday comfort.

  • How does the home feel in winter and summer?
  • What are the full recurring bills and up-front costs?
  • Is public transport usable for your actual schedule?
  • Has a qualified professional reviewed the agreement?
☼

Choosing your region

Pick a daily life, not just a pin on the map.

  • How does the town change outside tourism season?
  • Do you need a car for work, shopping or appointments?
  • How close are healthcare and essential services?
  • Will your language and community needs be met?
€

Build a real move budget

Keep the first months separate from ordinary ongoing costs.

  • Compare rent, deposits, temporary housing and moving costs
  • Allow for insurance, transport, utilities and unexpected repairs
  • Consider currency changes and a financial buffer
  • Ask a qualified tax adviser about your personal obligations
✓

Immigration information: verify it

Rules may change, and people's situations are different.

  • Check the relevant Portuguese consulate for your nationality
  • Consult the AIMA official website for residence information
  • Confirm documents and eligibility with qualified specialists
  • Never rely on a guaranteed approval or appointment claim
