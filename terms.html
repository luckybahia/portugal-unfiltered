Terms & service boundaries.

What Portugal Unfiltered does—and just as importantly, what it doesn't do.

Pre-launch template: These statements describe the intended service and prototype. Final consumer terms, cancellations, refunds, mandatory disclosures and business identification must be reviewed for Portuguese and applicable consumer law before taking payments.

1. Educational nature of information

Our website and intended services offer general, practical relocation education based on experience and publicly available information. They are not tailored legal, immigration, tax, financial, medical or property valuation advice.

2. Immigration and public authorities

We are not a Portuguese immigration authority, consulate or law firm. We do not assess legal eligibility, prepare or submit immigration applications or guarantee visas, residence permits, AIMA appointments, processing periods or results. Obtain specialist legal assistance when required and verify requirements with official sources.

3. Property services

We are not operating as a licensed Portuguese real-estate mediation agency. We do not source or broker properties, solicit parties for transactions, negotiate purchases or leases, collect brokerage commissions, or act on either side of a property deal. Property mediation in Portugal is regulated and requires licensing through IMPIC. Any discussion of listings or regions is educational, and you should retain licensed professionals for property transactions and legal review.

4. Pricing and contracting

Prices shown are indicative founding-stage package prices in euros. No online purchase, booking or contract is completed on this demonstration website. Before any commercial service is delivered, clients must receive confirmed service descriptions, final prices, applicable taxes, payment instructions and agreed terms.

5. Accuracy and external sources

Local conditions, policies and requirements change. We aim to distinguish personal observation from official requirements, but cannot guarantee that all information remains current or applies to every individual. Users should verify important facts with the appropriate authorities and independent qualified advisers.

6. Legal operator and consumer information

Before launch: add verified legal trading name, registered address, tax identification where required, customer support details, cancellation and refund policy, complaint channels and other mandatory consumer disclosures.
