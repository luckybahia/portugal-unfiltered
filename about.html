A note from the founder

Less sales talk. More useful truth.

I have spent nearly six years living in Portugal. Over time, I've seen how easily attractive images and well-intentioned online advice can miss the practical realities of relocation.

My aim with Portugal Unfiltered is to help future residents understand regions, ask smarter housing questions, prepare for everyday routines and know when to seek specialist help.

We are building this business from the ground up. We won't claim hundreds of clients, promise outcomes or pretend to be licensed in professions we don't practice.
