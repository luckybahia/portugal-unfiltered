Privacy notice.

How this website prototype currently handles information, and what must be completed before public launch.

Pre-launch notice: This is a sample privacy page, not a complete business-specific GDPR notice. Before collecting customer data, add the legal operator's identity, contact details, lawful bases, retention periods, processors, any transfers and other required information.

1. This demonstration website

The included contact form runs locally in your browser. Unless the site owner later configures a form endpoint, it does not transmit enquiry details to our server, send email, take payments or deliberately store the completed form in a database. The buttons let you copy or download what you typed on your own device.

2. Technical data

The hosting platform used by the eventual site owner may process technical connection data, such as IP addresses and server logs, under its own terms. The final operator must confirm which data the chosen hosting and form providers process.

3. Future contact and booking

When booking, email and payment services are connected, this notice must be expanded to explain the purposes, legal grounds, retention periods, service providers, international transfers and data-subject rights applicable to those operations.

4. Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable law, individuals may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to or port personal data, as well as to complain to Portugal's data protection authority, the CNPD.

5. Contact

Before launch: insert the verified legal business name, postal contact details, public email address and contact method here.
