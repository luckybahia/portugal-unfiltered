# Simple launch checklist

- [ ] Decide whether **Portugal Unfiltered** can be used as a trade name; verify domain and trademark conflicts.
- [ ] Choose and obtain your domain.
- [ ] Add your verified business name and required legal details.
- [ ] Set up a business email address.
- [ ] Update the founder bio with accurate details and optional real photo.
- [ ] Confirm final prices, service scopes and what is excluded.
- [ ] Review registration, permits and licensed-profession boundaries.
- [ ] Replace privacy and terms drafts with reviewed notices, disclosures and policies.
- [ ] Connect and test a secure enquiry form and spam protection.
- [ ] Connect calendar booking only when appointment slots and support workflow are ready.
- [ ] Connect payment provider only when invoicing, taxes, refunds and consumer disclosures are ready.
- [ ] Test the site on phone, tablet and desktop.
- [ ] Publish the site using a static website host and configure HTTPS.
- [ ] Send test enquiries, bookings and payments end-to-end before inviting customers.
- [ ] Share the website in relevant communities without spam or exaggerated promises.
